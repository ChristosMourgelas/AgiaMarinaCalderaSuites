Privacy Policy

Introduction

At Agia Marina Suites, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or make a reservation with us.

Information We Collect

We may collect several types of information from and about users of our website, including:

  • Personal Identity Data: Name, surname, and contact details provided via our contact form.
  • Booking Information: Dates of stay, number of guests, and preferences (often collected through third-party partners like Booking.com).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, time zone settings, and operating system, collected automatically through cookies.

How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To process and manage your reservations and stay.
  • To respond to inquiries and provide customer support.
  • To improve our website functionality and user experience.
  • To comply with legal obligations (e.g., tax regulations for hospitality).

Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses cookies to distinguish you from other users and to remember your preferences. You can manage your cookie settings through our consent banner or via your browser settings. For more information, please refer to our Cookie Policy within this document.

Third-Party Services

We may share your data with trusted third parties, such as booking engines or payment processors, only to the extent necessary to complete your transaction. We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal data. You may also object to the processing of your data at any time. To exercise these rights, please contact us at:
